Terms & Services

Last updated: April 25, 2025

Reservations

To guarantee your table, we highly recommend booking in advance. Early reservations help us prepare your preferred seating and accommodate any special requests. Should your plans change, please let us know at least 24 hours beforehand to avoid any cancellation fee and allow us to offer your spot to waiting guests.

Private Events

Our private dining experiences begin with a personalized consultation. A signed agreement and deposit secure your date, menu, and room layout. Final guest counts and menu choices are due one week prior. Cancellation policies and deposit refunds are detailed in your event contract.

Allergies & Dietary Needs

Please inform us of any food allergies, intolerances, or dietary preferences when you book or upon arrival. While we take utmost care in preparation, cross-contact can occur. Our chefs will gladly adapt dishes within reason to ensure a safe and enjoyable meal.

Service Charge & Tax

All checks include a 5% service charge which is shared among the team, and applicable government taxes. Should you wish to adjust service, please speak with your server. Gratuities beyond the service charge remain at your discretion.

Dress Code

We embrace smart-casual attire: collared shirts, tailored trousers, dresses, and dress shoes. Please avoid athletic wear or beachwear. Our goal is a refined yet approachable atmosphere for everyone’s enjoyment.

Conduct & Safety

In respect for all guests and staff, disruptive behavior or inappropriate language will not be tolerated. We reserve the right to refuse service or ask anyone to leave for safety or comfort reasons. Please follow any posted health or safety guidelines.

Photography & Content

By visiting, you consent to photographs or videos taken on the premises for our marketing and social media. If you prefer not to be featured, simply let your server know, and we will respectfully comply.

Changes to Terms
